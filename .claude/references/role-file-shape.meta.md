# Rationale: The role-file shape

**Audience:** whoever is changing a rule in `role-file-shape.md`. **Read when:** amending,
narrowing, or overturning one — never in order to follow one.

## Provenance

The user surfaced Gib Olander's JTBD+TROOP article on 2026-09-17 while shaping the
process-pipeline staffing (Task, Role, Output, Objective, Perspective, over a
Jobs-to-be-Done diagnostic layer; "if your agent doesn't have a real job, it's just
guessing"). The wider research the same day found the role-file anatomy already carried four
of TROOP's five letters under other names, with the Objective — the job the seat hires the
role to do — the one absent element. It also found independent corroboration in Medeiros's
Human-Agent Centered Design work: her intention-map contract fields (job served, success
outcomes, permissions and constraints, where human judgment remains, assumptions and risks)
map one-to-one onto write boundary, gates, sign-offs, and failure-direction prose the role
files already carry scattered, and her emerging roles — Intention Steward, Prompt Reviewer,
Sensemaker — reproduce the coach/writer/editor family shaped in conversation before the
literature was read.

## The incorporation ruling

User, 2026-09-17: **new roles only, plus this reference pair.** The three process roles are
authored JTBD-first with TROOP as the completeness checklist; the shape lands here so future
roles inherit it from a documented source. **No retrofit of the five existing role files** —
the rejected wider alternatives were retrofit-via-the-audit-sibling and
retrofit-plus-template; the audit sibling may cite this file when its own slice runs, but
this ruling does not reach it. The kind-neutrality rule earns its place on the audit
sibling's evidence: the five existing files state Story-shaped authority sources because
their hiring rationale was never written down, which is the drift class the job statement
dissolves by construction.

## The Vale probe, measured 2026-09-17

The architect's DESIGN pass probed the job-statement form before ruling no `vale-styles/`
change was needed. A throwaway agent file carrying all three drafted job statements under
the live `[.claude/agents/**/*.md]` section reported zero findings at warning level and zero
at suggestion level. The run was sentinel-verified: appending a 35-word sentence containing
"used to" and "previously" produced one `STE.SentenceLength` and two
`Instruction.HistoricalNarration` findings, so the zero was a measured zero rather than a
silent non-run. The probe file was deleted in the same pass.

## Why the emotional and social dimensions are excluded

Consumer JTBD carries three job dimensions because buyers do. A repo role has one hirer (the
seat, executing the user's rulings) and a functional job; an emotional or social dimension
written for one would be invented, and inventing it is the cargo-cult shape
`claim-discipline.md` exists to keep out of the corpus.
