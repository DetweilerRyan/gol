## Situation

CLAUDE.md's "Which files have a sidecar today" index lists both halves of the article tier
— articles that have one, articles that do not. Its role-file bullet lists only the five
that have one.

## Complication

Three roles now exist — `coach`, `writer`, `editor` — that the index neither includes nor
excludes. A reader cannot tell an omitted role from a role without a sidecar, so the index
cannot be read as complete, which is its one job.

## Question

Add the have-not half to the role-file bullet — or restate the bullet so it no longer
implies enumeration?

## Open questions

- The three process-role files are new and sidecar-less by design so far. Does that stay a
  fact the index records, or does `role-file-shape.md` owe them sidecars as their rulings
  accumulate?
