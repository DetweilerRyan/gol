# NNNN. Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted (not yet frozen) | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-….md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context and problem statement

What forces the decision, stated as a problem rather than a solution. Prefer measured
facts over inferred ones, and say which is which — an ADR is read later by someone who
cannot re-run the experiment.

## Decision drivers

The criteria the options are judged against. Naming these first is what stops the
Options section becoming a list of opinions.

## Options considered

One subsection per option, **including the ones rejected** — that is most of an ADR's
value. Each says what it is, what the evidence for or against it was, and why it lost.

## Decision

The option chosen, and the driver that settled it.

## Consequences

Positive, negative, and neutral. The negative ones are the point: an ADR that lists only
benefits is advocacy, not a record.

## Verification

What would falsify this decision, and what would have to be measured to revisit it.
