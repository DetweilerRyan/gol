## Situation

Child 1 of `prose-discipline-remedies.md`. The claim rules bind every prose surface and are
unmechanised by design; every mechanical gate can be green while a file violates them. Three
dated instances sit in the parent index.

## Complication

Applying the rules depends on authorial recall under a green-gates halo, and the seat that
writes the most instruction prose has no reviewer. The one-rulebook failure is measured: a
2026-09-15 repair pass fixed claim-discipline censuses by introducing two sidecar pointers,
the first shape on `prose.md`'s banned list.

## Question

What makes the claim rules run as a procedure on every prose landing, today, without waiting
for any Vale rule to exist?

## Answer

A `/prose-audit` skill on the `/idea-assess` shape: fork context, no Write, self-invocable by
the seat before landing prose. It reads **both rulebooks** — `claim-discipline.md` and
`prose.md`'s instruction-versus-explanation split — then the target, and returns findings with
the offending line quoted. Shaped, not specified; the skill's own rules follow at authoring.

## No-gos

- Not a reviewer. It narrows what a missing reviewer must catch; the reviewer question stays
  with `orchestrator-prose-has-no-reviewer.md`.
- Nothing gates. Findings report and route, per the standing ruling.
- No Vale authoring. The deterministic half is child 2's, and this child does not wait for it.

## Open questions

- Which surfaces by default — instruction files only, or every doc the landing touches?
- Should `/idea-promote` name a clean prose-audit as a precondition for the files a promotion
  edits, or does that begin to gate the board?
