work ruled a three-kinds triage for shared-surface role mentions — cross-role contracts stay, a
substitution table is a judgment, a restated duty goes — and `prose.meta.md` now carries that
table's evidence.

## Complication

against 24 at the 2026-09-11 measurement — one live contradiction was retired then, the triage

opens on its position in the sequence and enumerates all eight stages in order, the exact shape
`prose.md` bans from a description, which should state the precondition that makes the agent
the right one. The other four descriptions have not been checked.

## Question
